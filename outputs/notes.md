# Memorizing CFA Standards of Conduct Using the Memory-Palace Technique

## Summary

This section explains how to effectively memorize the 7 CFA standards of conduct and 22 substandards using a memory-palace technique combined with a narrative journey. It details how personal characteristics, the capital market, clients, employers, the investment process, conflicts of interest, and CFA responsibilities correspond to specific locations in the narrative. Through this spatial association, students can easily retain large amounts of ethics information for exams.
